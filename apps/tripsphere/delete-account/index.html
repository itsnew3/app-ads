Delete Your Tripsphere Account

You can request deletion of your Tripsphere account and associated app data using the instructions below.
Last updated: July 29, 2026

Delete Your Account in the App

The fastest way to delete your Tripsphere account is inside the Tripsphere mobile app:

  1. Open the Tripsphere app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Open My account.
  4. Tap Delete and confirm your request.

Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the app, contact support at:

friendsphere.app@icloud.com

Please send the request from the email address connected to your Tripsphere account when possible. We may ask for limited information only to verify that the account belongs to you.

What Data Is Deleted

When your Tripsphere account data is deleted, we remove app data associated with your account, including:

  • Your Tripsphere profile
  • Your profile photos and uploaded Tripsphere photos
  • Your trips and travel companion listings
  • Your travel tips and tip photos
  • Your chats and messages
  • Your likes, saved items, blocks, notifications, and app usage records related to Tripsphere features

Some limited records may be retained only if required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or dispute resolution.

Processing Time

In-app deletion is processed immediately when possible. Email requests are reviewed and processed within a reasonable period, usually within 30 days.

Contact
