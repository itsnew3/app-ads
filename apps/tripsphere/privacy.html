Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Tripsphere collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use our mobile application.
Last updated: June 21, 2026

1. Who We Are

Tripsphere is operated by the Tripsphere team. We are committed to respecting your privacy and complying with applicable data protection regulations.

2. Age Requirement

Tripsphere is intended for users aged 18 years and older. Users under 18 are not allowed to use the App. If we discover an account that violates this policy, we will delete it.

3. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide and improve our services:

  • Basic information such as name, email, gender, and date of birth
  • Profile photos and additional profile details
  • Travel plans, trip details, travel tips, likes, saved items, and messages
  • Location information you provide through profile fields, trips, tips, or place search
  • Usage and device data for analytics and support

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Help you find travel companions and discover travel tips
  • Display your profile, trips, tips, photos, and activity according to your privacy settings
  • Provide messaging, saved items, and support features
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve app functionality and performance
  • Offer relevant features or promotions

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We only share your data with:

  • Service providers, such as cloud storage, analytics, and verification tools
  • Moderators to ensure content compliance
  • Law enforcement when required by law

6. Advertising

We may display ads based on limited anonymized data. You can opt out of targeted ads in the app settings or through your device settings where applicable.

7. Cookies and Device Data

We may use cookies and similar technologies for app functionality and performance. You can manage permissions through your device settings.

8. Data Retention

We retain user data while your account is active. Upon deletion, data is removed within 30 days, except when retention is required for fraud prevention, security, or legal reasons.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure. Please keep your credentials private.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Correct your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to data processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time

You can exercise these rights through the Support section in the App or by contacting us.

11. Children's Privacy

Users under 18 are not allowed. We delete accounts found to violate this policy.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated within the App.

13. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, you can contact us via the Support section in the App or by email:
